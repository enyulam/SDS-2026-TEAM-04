## 0. Amendment 004 reconciliation (2026-08-05) — read before using any row below

**The report workflow this matrix was originally written against has been superseded.** Amendment 004 ratified a **two-stage** workflow, and every row and porting action in this document has been reconciled to it.

**The governing workflow is now:**

1. The trainer completes the assessment; AI drafts; the trainer reviews and edits.
2. **The trainer approves the exact trainer-reviewed version.** Trainer approval **does not publish** and does not make anything parent-visible.
3. Management is notified and performs the **final quality review**.
4. Management may **approve unchanged**, **edit only the four parent-facing wording panels** (Today's Strength, Next Focus, Practice Suggestion, Session Takeaway), or **return the report to the trainer** — the last **only** when a rating, an observation, or a substantive assessment fact appears incorrect.
5. **The trainer owns every substantive correction** and **must reapprove** the corrected version. Management never edits a rating, observation, attendance record, evidence object or trainer note.
6. **Management performs the final Approve & Submit**, which is the only action that publishes.
7. **Parents see only the submitted canonical version.**

**Four instructions that appeared in earlier revisions of this matrix are superseded and must not be implemented** (Amendment 004 A-033, A-034, A-038):

| Superseded instruction | Superseded by |
|---|---|
| Management is **report-view-only** | **A-034** — management edits parent-facing wording, returns reports, and approves |
| Management has **no report-edit affordance** / an edit affordance must be absent | **A-034** — management has a **bounded** wording-only edit affordance; what is server-enforced is the **field boundary**, not a blanket denial |
| **Trainer approval directly publishes** | **A-033** — trainer approval moves the report to `trainer_approved`; publication is management's |
| **Approve & Submit is trainer-only** | **A-033** — "Approve & Submit" is **management's** action; the trainer's action is **"Approve"** |

**What is unchanged and still absolute:** the parent boundary (view-only, submitted-only, linked students only, no per-dimension rating grid in any form); AI never publishes; the trainer's three-item Quality Checklist gates **trainer approval** and is never a management instrument; every governed write stays server-side, centre-scoped, validated, authorized and auditable.
