- **Current implementation status** uses the ratified classification vocabulary: `Implemented and visually aligned` (used by no screen), `Implemented but visually unaligned`, `Partially implemented`, `Not implemented`.

- **Route compatibility.** Several implemented routes differ from their canonical route. The per-screen treatment is recorded in each `screen.md` section 1 and comes from the ratified inventory section 7. **Executing a treatment requires its own authorization; this pack creates no route.**
- **ID 05 Trainer Schedule has no implemented route at all** - session selection is currently folded into the trainer landing surface. Its treatment is `Operator decision required` (inventory section 7.3), and this pack does not make that decision.
