# Management - Reports

## Screen purpose

- Provides Management with school-wide oversight of individual Student Reports and Term Reports.
- Allows Management to find reports and distinguish approved reports from reports awaiting approval.

## Page sections and UI components

- Management Portal sidebar.
- **Reports** heading and school-wide oversight description.
- Filters for term, class, and status.
- Student search field.
- Reports table.
- Columns for Student, Class, Lesson, Trainer, Submitted, and Status.
- **View report** and **Review** row actions.
- Notification icon and signed-in Management profile.

## What each section displays

- The filters show the current term, class, and status scope.
- The table displays each Student report’s Class, Lesson, Trainer, submission date, and approval status.
- Approved rows show a green **Approved** badge and **View report** action.
- Pending rows show a **Needs approval** badge and **Review** action.

## User interactions

- Filter reports by term, class, or status.
- Search for a student.
- Open an approved report.
- Review a report that needs approval.
- Use the sidebar to open another management section.

## Button and navigation behavior

- Filter controls update the visible report rows.
- The search field narrows the list by student.
- **View report** opens the selected approved report; only Management may edit a report after it has been sent to a Parent.
- **Review** opens the selected report for management review and approval.
- **Logout** returns to the management login screen.
